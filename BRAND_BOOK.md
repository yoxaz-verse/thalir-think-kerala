# Thalir by Think Kerala — Brand Book v1.0

## Brand idea

Thalir means a new sprout: something rooted, alive and full of possibility. The brand connects founders, investors, mentors, institutions and partners without presenting itself as a gatekeeper. Its promise is simple: **where bold ideas take root**.

Thalir carries forward Think Kerala's versatile, optimistic and politically neutral outlook. Its own operating principles are **rooted, enabling, inclusive and credible**.

## Identity

The symbol combines a sprout with connected nodes. The central stem represents grounded progress; the leaves represent ideas and reciprocal exchange; the nodes make the ecosystem visible. The primary lockup reads “thalir” with the endorsement “by Think Kerala.”

- Keep clear space equal to one circular node around every edge.
- Minimum width: 120 px for the lockup and 24 px for the standalone symbol.
- Use Forest on light backgrounds, Leaf on Forest, or a single-colour white version when required.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, add effects, recolour individual parts, or place over visually busy/low-contrast imagery.

## Typography

- **Newsreader Variable:** English display and editorial moments. Its humanist contrast adds authority without institutional coldness.
- **Figtree Variable:** English interface, labels and body copy.
- **Noto Sans Malayalam Variable:** Malayalam interface, display and body copy. Never force Latin tracking rules onto Malayalam.
- Prefer sentence case. Reserve uppercase and letter spacing for short English metadata labels.

## Voice and bilingual writing

Thalir is specific, generous and grounded. Explain what a program does, who it helps and what it cannot promise. Avoid inflated claims, startup clichés, unexplained English jargon and literal Malayalam translations that sound bureaucratic.

- Say: “Bring your model and questions. We’ll help map the next useful step.”
- Avoid: “Unlock limitless success with Kerala’s ultimate innovation revolution.”
- English and Malayalam should carry the same intent, not necessarily identical sentence structure.
- Write Kerala place names using familiar contemporary forms and review public Malayalam with a fluent editor before launch.

## Imagery and illustration

Show real work, place and participation: workshops, campuses, small teams, makers, founders listening, and useful encounters. Represent Kerala through lived texture—coasts, laterite, shade, towns and working spaces—rather than tourism clichés. Use warm natural light, honest colour and negative space for typography. Obtain documented permission for identifiable people.

Abstract graphics use leaf forms, connected nodes, contours and circular fields. Icons are simple line forms with rounded terminals.

## UI and accessibility

Components use a 4/8 px spacing rhythm, restrained radii and motion under 250 ms. Keyboard focus must always remain visible. Never encode meaning in colour alone. Honour reduced-motion settings, use semantic headings, provide descriptive link labels and keep interactive targets at least 44 px.

## Applications

Use the complete lockup for the website header, partner material and formal documents. The symbol alone is appropriate for avatars and favicon use. Social messages lead with one grounded idea and a short source/next step. Campaign layouts use one strong statement, generous space, one organic field and the endorsement.

The live `/en/brand` and `/ml/brand` pages demonstrate approved digital usage.
